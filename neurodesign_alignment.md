# BLOOM NeuroDesign Alignment Report

## Human Brain <-> Robot Brain
BLOOM uses a co-adaptive reinforcement learning loop that updates response preferences from implicit next-frame feedback. Improvements in face gesture, body regulation, and gaze centering become reward signals, allowing the robot policy to track the child's cognitive-emotional state over time.

## Human Brain <-> Robot Body
Visual and behavioral cues (eye gaze, face gesture, body language, spinning type, age) are transformed into engagement scores and context-aware affirming language. BLOOM's verbal output and optional TTS express regulation-aware support without compliance pressure.

## Robot Brain <-> Human Body
The robot selects low-pressure, neuroaffirming responses to reduce distress and support self-regulation. Engagement trend detection (rising/stable/falling/crisis) helps tune interaction pacing and language intensity to the child's arousal state.

## Human Body <-> Robot Body
Stim-pattern recognition models onset, sustained, and post-stim phases from rolling body-language windows. Physical signals from the child directly influence robot behavior, producing phase-specific responses that validate movement as meaningful self-regulation.

## Competition Criteria Mapping
- NeuroDesign alignment: explicit four-loop brain-body-robot coupling with adaptive feedback.
- Real-world impact: session persistence, child baseline modeling, and personalization growth across repeated interactions.
- Innovation: lightweight online RL + multimodal engagement prediction + stim-phase-aware language policy.
- Brain-centered experience design: supportive, autonomy-preserving, non-corrective interaction framework for autistic children.
